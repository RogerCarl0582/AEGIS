**Principle:** You cannot cure indigestion by eating more food.
**The Book's Law:** Stop external input to force the body to switch fuel sources.

**Action Plan: The Procurement Guillotine**
*   **The Decree:** Initiate a "Zero-Buy" period. For a set cycle (e.g., 2 weeks or 1 month), **absolutely no new inventory** enters the warehouse.

# 🌊 Phase 2: Inducing Autophagy (The Liquidation Engine)
**Principle:** When glucose (cash) is low, the body targets garbage cells and visceral fat to burn for fuel.
**The Book's Law:** "Recycle the waste."

We need to turn your "Dead Stock" (Fat) back into "Cash" (Energy), even if the efficiency is lower than usual.

### **Derivative Strategy A: The "Mystery Box" Detox (Blind Box Logic)**
*   **Concept:** The body doesn't discriminate when burning fat; it cleans house.
*   **Application:** Create "Fuku-bukuro" (Lucky Bags) or Mystery Boxes.
*   **Why it works:**
    *   Group high-desirability items (The Hook) with severe dead-stock items (The Waste).
    *   Sell the bundle at a price that covers the cost of goods + shipping + small margin.
    *   **Result:** You are physically removing the "bloating" (goods) and converting it into "oxygen" (cash) rapidly. You sacrifice high profit margins for **survival cash flow**.

### **Derivative Strategy B: The "Calories for Heat" Sale (Flash Liquidation)**
*   **Concept:** Thermogenesis. Burning energy just to stay warm.
*   **Application:** A "Warehouse Cleansing" Live Stream Event.
*   **The Narrative:** Be honest. "We are stuffed. We need to fast. Our 'Indigestion' is your gain."
*   **Mechanism:** Deep discounts (50-70% off).
*   **AEGIS Note:** Do not worry about "brand devaluation" at this stage. A dead organism has no brand value. Survival is priority $P_0$.

### **Derivative Strategy C: Nutrient Extraction (Bundling)**
*   **Concept:** Biology breaks down complex proteins into amino acids to build new things.
*   **Application:** Dismantle existing SKUs.
    *   If you have a slow-selling "Camping Set," break it apart. Sell the flashlight alone. Sell the tent alone. Or bundle the flashlight with a fast-selling battery pack.
    *   **Recontextualize the item:** Change the main image and the description. The product isn't bad; its *presentation* (enzymes) failed to digest it.
